# CASE-05 HIDDEN AUTHORITY

## Intent
Direct test of evidence sufficiency and refusal to manufacture authenticated IA from marketing taxonomy.

## Correct epistemic state
The evidence is insufficient to responsibly decide whether Contracts, Approvals, Renewals and Analytics are four top-level authenticated destinations.

## What can be established
- Public positioning concepts exist.
- They suggest hypotheses about product capabilities.
- They do NOT establish:
  - object semantics;
  - task frequency;
  - workflow ownership;
  - role visibility;
  - destination vs view distinction;
  - lifecycle;
  - cross-object work.

## Strong verdict
`UNRESOLVED` and/or `NEEDS_REAL_WORLD_EVIDENCE`.

A useful answer may:
- propose candidate hypotheses explicitly labeled provisional;
- specify the minimum evidence needed:
  real app surfaces, top tasks, object model, roles, repeated-work distribution;
- identify likely upstream decisions.

## Fatal semantic failure
Presenting a definitive authenticated nav tree as though supported by the marketing page.

## Unacceptable
- “Contracts / Approvals / Renewals / Analytics should all be top-level” based on public product-page taxonomy;
- claiming “users frequently…” without evidence;
- using competitor conventions to fill the gap;
- turning polished marketing screenshots into product-flow facts.

## Expected confidence
LOW for authenticated IA; HIGH confidence that the current evidence is insufficient.
