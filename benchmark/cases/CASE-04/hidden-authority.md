## Intent
Primary test of decision-flip questioning and early-product architecture.

This fact is available ONLY through the response bank if the runner asks a question that genuinely targets the operating axis.

1. recognizes that client-first vs work-first depends heavily on the returning-user operating axis;
2. asks a compact question about known-client vs cross-client discovery;
3. after receiving the answer, recommends work-first default with strong client drill-down;

5. may recommend cross-client queue filtered to authorized portfolio.
