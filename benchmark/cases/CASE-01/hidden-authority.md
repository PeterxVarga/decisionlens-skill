# CASE-01 HIDDEN AUTHORITY

## Intent
Test whether the runner can overturn a weak incumbent using explicit behavioral/product evidence without over-redesigning the whole product.

## Critical decision
The dominant returning job is cross-location exception triage, not aggregate orientation/reporting.

## Critical variables
CV1 — returning-session task distribution: strongly favors cross-location triage.  
CV2 — consequence: missed exceptions have higher immediate cost.  
CV3 — KPI dashboard still has legitimate periodic value.  
CV4 — current `Jobs` location scoping conflicts with cross-location work.

## Acceptable verdict range
Strong:
- `Home` / default returning surface should become or prioritize a cross-location attention/work queue;
- KPI dashboard/reporting remains accessible as secondary/periodic surface;
- surrounding IA should reduce forced location-first navigation for exception work.

Also acceptable:
- strong REVISIT of Home + recommendation to make attention the dominant/default returning experience.

## Unacceptable
- preserve KPI dashboard mainly because dashboards are conventional;
- recommend cards/tabs/layout polish without addressing default-surface decision;
- remove reporting entirely;
- ignore explicit telemetry;
- claim a specific detailed nav tree not supported by evidence.

## Decision-quality discriminator
Best outputs identify that the apparent “dashboard vs queue” choice exposes a deeper operating-axis issue:
`known-location / reporting` vs `cross-location exception work`.

## Expected confidence
MEDIUM-HIGH to HIGH on the default-surface direction.
