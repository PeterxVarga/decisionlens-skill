copyright similarity analysis of third-party work has **not** been performed.

evidence sufficiency, decision-flip questions, adversarial alternative
comparison, non-convergence outcomes and explicit reopen conditions.

or adoption. Wave 02 falsified V1; the V0 runtime was preserved.
