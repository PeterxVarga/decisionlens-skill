# Public Claims Contract

## Safe headline claim

> In a frozen 6-case blind hostile bake-off, Product Architecture Review V0 materially outperformed the preselected case-matched public comparator on 5 of 6 cases, with zero evidence-integrity failures.

## Safe supporting claims

- 18/18 executions completed validly.
- PAR passed all 6 targeted behavior tests.
- PAR materially outperformed the baseline model on 4/6 cases.
- The strongest separation appeared on decision-flip questioning, evidence sufficiency/refusal, and upstream reframing.
- The exact model-visible V0 runtime is byte-identical to the candidate used in the bake-off.

## Claims to avoid

Do **not** say:

- “PAR is objectively better than UX skills.”
- “PAR is 99% accurate.”
- “PAR finds the objectively best IA.”
- “PAR is proven on real products.”
- “PAR beats every senior product design skill.”
- “The benchmark proves market demand.”

## Why

Wave 01 is a controlled falsification of a specific methodology thesis.

It is intentionally not:
- a broad UX benchmark;
- a human-evaluator study;
- a real-world adoption study;
- an exhaustive competitor tournament.

The bounded claim is stronger because it is auditable.
