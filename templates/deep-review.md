# PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE REVIEW — DEEP

## 1. Scope and evidence boundary
- supplied surfaces/context
- what was observed
- what cannot be inferred

## 2. Minimum product model
- actors
- dominant jobs
- recurring jobs
- consequential objects
- actions
- constraints

## 3. Current architecture diagnosis
Describe mechanisms, not a generic issue list.

## 4. Decision register

For each consequential decision:

### D-XXX — Question
- Risk
- Why consequential
- Candidate A
- Candidate B
- Evidence ledger
- Decision-flip variables
- Research used
- Strongest attack
- Status
- Preferred direction (if any)
- Confidence
- Reopen condition

## 5. Recommended architecture
Synthesize only supported/recommended decisions.
Clearly mark unresolved/reframed dependencies.

## 6. Research and precedent
Only decision-relevant external research.
Separate external guidance from product-specific evidence.

## 7. Real-world evidence plan
Only when unresolved decisions require it.

## 8. Reopen map
Which future evidence/conditions would reopen which decisions.
